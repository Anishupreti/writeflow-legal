WriteFlow AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

The short version: WriteFlow has no server of its own. Everything it stores stays on your own device. The only place your data goes is directly from your browser to whichever AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini) you've configured, using your own API key — and only when you actively ask WriteFlow to generate something.

What WriteFlow AI does

WriteFlow AI is a browser extension that adds an AI writing assistant to text fields across the web. It rewrites, drafts replies to, and analyzes context for text you're working with, using an AI provider you choose and authenticate with your own API key.

What is stored, and where

All of the following is stored locally in this browser via the standard chrome.storage.local API. None of it is synced to a WriteFlow server, because none exists.

What is sent to AI providers, specifically

When you use a writing command, WriteFlow sends: the text you selected or the field's current content; for context-aware replies, the extracted post or email text being replied to; your writing samples/style notes, if set; and the instruction for what you asked WriteFlow to do.

This is sent directly from your browser to the API endpoint of the provider you've configured (api.openai.com, api.anthropic.com, or generativelanguage.googleapis.com), authenticated with your own API key. WriteFlow does not proxy, log, or otherwise see this traffic.

Your relationship with that provider is governed by their own privacy policy and terms, not this one.

What is never collected

WriteFlow does not collect: analytics, telemetry, browsing history, your identity, or any data beyond what's described above. There is no tracking of any kind.

Your controls

  • Disable WriteFlow on specific sites — popup "Disable on this site," or the Disabled Sites list in Settings.
  • Clear feedback history — Settings → Privacy & Data → "Clear feedback history."
  • Delete all local data — Settings → Privacy & Data → "Delete all local data." Immediately and permanently clears everything, including your API key and settings.
  • Uninstall — removes all locally stored data with it.

Children's privacy

WriteFlow is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If WriteFlow's data handling changes, this document will be updated and the date above will reflect that.
