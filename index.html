FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

Do I need an account or an API key to try it?

No account, ever. To try it without a key, leave Settings → Provider on Mock: every feature returns sample results and nothing is sent anywhere. For real results, add your own key. Google Gemini's free tier in Google AI Studio is the easiest place to start.

Which AI providers are supported?

OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google Gemini, Groq and OpenRouter. You choose the provider and the models you want to use. Your key is stored only in this browser.

What is ✨ Improve, and how many can I use for free?

In ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, type a prompt of six or more words and click ✨ Improve beside the message box, or press Alt+Shift+I. Your AI provider rewrites it into a clearer prompt, and you choose whether to use it. You get 100 free Improves to start, then 20 free every month. Errors and cancelled ones don't count. Pro makes Improve unlimited, and every other feature stays free.

Where does my text go?

When you ask for a result, the text you're working on, plus the post or email you're replying to for replies, goes directly from your browser to your AI provider, using your key. WriteFlow has no server and never sees it. Prompt checks run on your device. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Reply options don't appear on a social site. What do I check?
  • Click inside the comment box first, then click the WriteFlow button.
  • If WriteFlow can't read the post, the reply panel asks you to paste it. Your reply is still tailored.
  • Check that the site isn't turned off in the popup or in Settings → Disabled Sites.
  • Still wrong? Email the site and what you clicked, and the layout will be fixed.
Does Job Assist apply for me?

No. It reads the job, fills the form fields you choose and attaches your CV, and it can draft answers and a cover letter. You check everything and press the site's own submit button yourself. Your CV and profile stay in your browser, and your name and contact details are never sent to the AI.

Which browsers does WriteFlow work in?

Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Firefox on desktop. Your settings and data stay in the browser you use; nothing is synced between browsers except the free-Improve counter through your browser's own sync.

Will WriteFlow send or post anything for me?

Never. Every result waits for you to choose Use it, Insert, Replace or Copy. Prompts in AI chats are never sent for you.

Is Pro a subscription?

Yes. Pro is a Gumroad membership at $4.99 a month, and it starts with a 7-day free trial. Gumroad checks your card at signup but charges nothing until day 8, and WriteFlow reminds you 2 days before. You can cancel any time from your Gumroad library, and Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Your price may include VAT or sales tax for your country. If you bought the original one-time Pro licence, it keeps working and you never need to subscribe.

Generation fails or shows an error. What's wrong?

Most often the selected provider doesn't match the key. For example, an OpenAI key won't work with Anthropic selected. Also check that the key hasn't expired or run out of credit. See Support for more.
