Support

WriteFlow has no server, no account and no login. The steps below fix most problems. If they don't, email and you'll get a reply from the developer, not a ticket queue.

Getting started

  • Try it without a key. Settings → AI and models starts on Demo mode: click into any text box, click the WriteFlow button, and try every feature with sample results. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Add your own key for real results: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter. Paste it in Settings. The Gemini free tier from Google AI Studio needs no card.
  • Pick your models in Settings → AI and models → Advanced: models. WriteFlow only uses and suggests the models you enable there.
  • Write and reply: click inside a text box, then click the WriteFlow button, or press Ctrl+Shift+Space (⌘+Shift+Space on Mac).
  • Improve a prompt: in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, type six or more words and click ✨ Improve, or press Alt+Shift+I.
  • Apply for a job: add your CV once on the Job Assist page (popup → Job Assist). On a job ad or application form, open the popup and press Read this job, or right-click the page and choose Read this job with Job Assist.
  • Track your applications: every job you read, fill or apply to appears in the Job Tracker (popup → Job Assist → Job Tracker tab).
  • Save something to review: select text on any page, right-click, and choose Save selection to Review.

Troubleshooting

  • The WriteFlow button doesn't appear. Check the popup and Settings → Features → Sites where WriteFlow is off. For a site that isn't supported yet, click Enable WriteFlow here in the popup. Search boxes, passwords, PIN and code fields, sign-in forms and payment fields are left alone on purpose.
  • Job Assist shows the wrong job title or company. Fix it in the Job Assist panel before you write; or press Read again with AI to let your AI read the job from the page.
  • My CV isn't read. Use a PDF, Word (.docx), ODT, RTF or text file. Scanned PDFs and old .doc files have no readable text: save as .docx or PDF from Word, or paste the text instead.
  • Social reply shows a paste box. WriteFlow couldn't read the post on that page. Paste it and your reply is still tailored. Please email the site and what you clicked so the layout can be supported.
  • "Replying to" shows the wrong text. Click Not right? Edit and fix it before choosing a reply style.
  • ✨ Improve doesn't show. It appears for drafts of six or more words in ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. Check that the Improve chip is turned on in Settings, and that a provider and key are set.
  • "Free Improves used up." Your free Improves are used up for now; 20 more arrive each month. Every other feature keeps working, and Pro makes Improve unlimited.
  • Generation fails or shows an error. Make sure the provider matches the key (an OpenAI key won't work with Anthropic selected), the model is enabled, and the key has credit.
  • Pro isn't unlocking. Paste the full licence key with no spaces, while online. Verification is done by Gumroad.
  • Something looks stuck. Reload the page. If it still happens, Settings → Privacy and data → Delete all local data resets WriteFlow and deletes everything it stored, including your Job Tracker. Export your Job Tracker (CSV) and Review Workspace first if you use them.

Privacy

Your key and settings stay on your device. Text goes only to the AI provider you set up, and only when you ask for a result. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
